About Our Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood-focused bakery built around the joy of sharing freshly baked bread and pastries. We celebrate everyday visits, family gatherings, and special occasions with small-batch baking.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Our approach emphasizes simple ingredients, local grains when available, and the time needed to make flavorful bread. Ask our team about ingredients and allergy considerations before placing an order.

Meet the Team

Our bakers prepare bread and pastries with care, while our front-of-house team helps neighbors choose treats and plan pre-orders.
